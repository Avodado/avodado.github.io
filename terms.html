Terms of Use

This website describes a personal desktop utility. It does not offer public accounts, mailbox access, order processing, subscriptions, or a hosted monitoring service.

Application scope

FRB Order Monitor is operated privately by its owner on their own computer and authorized accounts. The operator is responsible for choosing which accounts to authorize, reviewing unmatched or ambiguous order evidence, and verifying picking and shipping decisions against the relevant marketplaces.

A local Pick List is an operational aid, not a guarantee of current marketplace status. A notification alone may not establish an exact inventory match. The monitor does not replace marketplace terms, payment records, or shipment confirmation.

Separate services

Google, GitHub, eBay, Poshmark, Depop, and Vendoo are separate services with their own terms and policies. This application is not endorsed by or affiliated with those services. Naming them explains the workflow and integrations.

Privacy and contact

Read the Privacy Policy for Google data access, local storage, and revocation controls. The site is maintained through the Avodado GitHub account. Do not submit private customer, order, or authentication information through public GitHub activity.
